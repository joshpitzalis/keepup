# Experimental interface-leverage rubric v1

This reviewed judgement does not establish scientific validity or learner understanding.
Freeze representative scenario identities and meanings before comparing results.

- 0: callers reconstruct the workflow or own internal policy.
- 0.5: the module owns the workflow but callers manage avoidable internal details.
- 1: callers express intent and handle the meaningful outcome through a coherent interface.

Average the fixed scenario inventory only when every scenario has a rating and concrete evidence.
Show domain and avoidable caller-obligation counts beside the average. Ratings require an
explanation; unknowns remain unavailable. An empty inventory remains unavailable. Provisional
judgements stay labelled provisional. A single untyped catch-all operation does not establish
coherent intent or meaningful outcome handling. More implementation lines do not improve a rating.
